Resident FAQ

The questions our leasing office is asked most. Anything not covered here, call (602) 555-0148 — someone picks up between 8.30 and 5.30, Monday to Friday.

Applying

What does it cost to apply?

The application fee is $45 per adult applicant. It covers credit and background screening and is non-refundable, including if the application is declined.

What do I need to provide?

Photo ID, your two most recent pay stubs or a signed offer letter, and contact details for your two most recent landlords. Self-employed applicants can provide the last two years of returns instead.

How long does screening take?

Two to three business days once we have a complete file. Incomplete files are the usual cause of delay — most often a missing landlord reference.

Do you accept co-signers?

Yes, on most homes. A co-signer completes the same application and pays the same $45 fee, and must show income of at least four times the monthly rent.

What income do you look for?

Combined gross household income of three times the monthly rent, verifiable through pay stubs, an offer letter or tax returns. We look at the whole file rather than a single score.

Rent & payments

When is rent due, and how do I pay it?

Rent is due on the first and considered late after the third. Pay through the resident portal by bank transfer or card, or by cheque at the office. Portal bank transfers carry no fee.

What happens if I am going to be late?

Call us before the third rather than after. We would far rather agree a short arrangement in advance than start the notice process, and we have never charged a resident who told us early.

Which utilities am I responsible for?

Electricity and internet in every home. Water, sewer and refuse are included at Camelback Terrace and Scottsdale Ranch, and billed back at the other communities — the listing states which applies.

Living here

How do I report a maintenance issue?

Through the resident portal, or call the office. Anything affecting cooling, heat, water or the security of your home is treated as urgent and dispatched the same day, including weekends.

In a Phoenix summer we treat a failed air conditioner as an emergency at any hour. Call rather than submit a ticket.

Are pets allowed?

At most of our communities, with a pet addendum. Weight limits and breed restrictions vary by property — the listing states the policy for that home. Assistance animals are not pets and carry no fee.

Can I paint, mount a TV, or change the fixtures?

Ask first and the answer is usually yes. Mounting hardware and paint colours we have approved in writing are not charged against your deposit at move-out; unapproved changes are.

Where do guests park?

Marked visitor bays at every community except Ironwood Crossing, where street parking on the loop is unrestricted. Assigned resident spaces are enforced and vehicles are towed after a warning.

Moving out

How much notice do I need to give?

Thirty days in writing before the end of the lease term. We send a renewal offer roughly sixty days out, so there is time to decide either way.

How is my deposit handled?

We complete a documented move-out inspection, room by room. Normal wear and tear is never charged. Anything chargeable is itemised with a photo reference, and the balance is returned within 14 days of possession.

Can I be present at the inspection?

Yes, and we recommend it. Ask when you give notice and we will book the walk-through for a time you can attend, so anything disputed gets settled in the room rather than by email a fortnight later.
