What we handle

One team for leasing, maintenance and the monthly accounting close — so nothing sits waiting between two inboxes.

  • Leasing & applications

    Listing, showings and screening, with applications reviewed in the order received. The application fee is $45 per adult applicant, collected up front and non-refundable.

  • Maintenance & vendors

    Vetted local trades on standing rates. Anything touching cooling, heat, water or the security of a home is dispatched the same day, weekends included, and every unit keeps its own repair history.

  • Owner accounting

    A full month-end close per property on a published schedule — bank and deposit reconciliations, AR and AP, accruals, then a trial balance reviewed before anything is locked.

  • Move-outs & deposits

    A documented room-by-room inspection with photographs. Normal wear and tear is never charged, chargeable items are itemised against a photo, and the balance returns within 14 days of possession.

Thinking about handing over the keys?

Send us the address and last year's statements. We will come back with a written proposal — projected rent, the work a turn would need, and what our fee actually covers — usually inside three business days.
