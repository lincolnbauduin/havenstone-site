Request a proposal

Your statement should not need a phone call to explain it.

We manage 166 homes across six Valley communities. Owners get one team for leasing, maintenance and accounting, and a month-end close that runs on a published schedule whether or not anyone chases it.

How it works

From first call to first statement is usually four to six weeks, most of which is the turn work rather than the paperwork.

  1. Walk the property

    We visit, photograph the condition and tell you plainly what a turn would need before the home can be listed at the rent we are quoting.

  2. Written proposal

    Projected rent against comparable homes we actually manage, the fee schedule below, and an estimate for any work identified. Usually within three business days.

  3. Turn and list

    Vendors on standing rates do the make-ready. Photography, listing and showings follow, with applications screened in the order received.

  4. Close the month, every month

    Bank and deposit reconciliations, receivables, payables and accruals, then a trial balance reviewed before the period is locked. Statements land by the fifth working day.

Fees

Two line items, both stated up front. We do not mark up vendor invoices, and we do not take a share of anything we charge a resident.

Management

Charged on rent actually collected — not on rent invoiced.

8% of monthly rent

  • Rent collection and arrears follow-up
  • Maintenance coordination and vendor management
  • Monthly close and owner statement
  • Annual statement for your accountant

Leasing

Charged once, when a signed lease starts.

50% of one month's rent

  • Photography and listing across the major portals
  • Showings and applicant screening
  • Lease preparation and execution
  • Documented move-in inspection

What we never charge

Stated because these are the ones owners get caught by elsewhere.

Nil

  • No setup or onboarding fee
  • No vacancy fee while a home is empty
  • No mark-up on vendor invoices
  • No fee to leave — thirty days' notice
